How Could There Be Life When There Is Death? Lessons from Grief

This blog is about a dream that keeps visiting me, the people I've lost, and a conversation with a grieving mother that I can't stop thinking about. I don't have the answers, but I'd like to share what I've learned so far. Writing about it is my way of making sense of grief.

The dream

On a random day, I came across a young kid who kept asking an old man, "Pwede ta mo laag menteryo?" It sounded strange. I wondered why a child would choose the cemetery over the plaza.

Then I saw them walking together, each holding a stick broom and a small brush, heading into the cemetery. At the entrance, they stopped at a tomb, and I assumed a family member was laid to rest there. From the corner of my eye, I saw them light candles, then sweep and brush the tomb. The kid was so happy and innocent, as if the place were her playground.

I left. After that, the kid and the old man began visiting me in my dreams.
